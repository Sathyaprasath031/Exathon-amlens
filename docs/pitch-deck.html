Slide 03 · Gap Analysis Why Existing Rules Fail

Why Traditional Thresholds Break Down

1. 97%+ False Positives

Threshold rules like "Alert on cash >= ₹10 Lakhs" repeatedly flag innocent businesses, local merchants, and high-net-worth customers depositing ordinary revenue.

2. Blind to Layering

Launderers rarely send ₹15 Lakhs in one shot. They structure deposits under ₹50k, pass funds through mule accounts, or route money in loops across multiple banks.

3. Zero Graph Context

Traditional rules evaluate a transaction in total isolation. They cannot see the money velocity, fan-in collectors, or cycles in the money-flow graph.

Slide 04 · Solution Five Typologies

Five Complex Typologies, One SQL Engine

1. Structuring (Smurfing)

Detects multiple deposits clustered just below the ₹50,000 threshold within a 7-day rolling window.

2. Pass-Through Routing

Flags accounts receiving incoming funds and immediately draining >= 85% within 48 hours with minimal retention.

3. Fan-In Mule Collectors

Discovers mule hubs receiving incoming transfers from 5+ distinct accounts and centralizing the balance.

4. Circular Flow (Wash Loops)

Performs recursive graph self-joins to catch closed money loops (Account A -> B -> C -> A) obscuring origins.

5. Segment-Aware Income Mismatch

Flags accounts whose transactional velocity exceeds 300% of their declared monthly income, calibrated differently for retail vs. business segments.

Slide 08 · Conclusion Roadmap & Limitations

Honest Boundaries & Next Steps

Future Roadmap

  • Integrate Kafka streaming directly into Exasol tables for near real-time ingestion.
  • Incorporate analyst feedback loop to dynamically adjust typology weights based on audit outcomes.
  • Expand to trade-based money laundering and international wire typologies.

Honest Limitations

  • Synthetic Data: Results validate the algorithmic pipeline and SQL logic; real bank environments will exhibit more noise.
  • Decision Support: AMLens assists and prioritizes; a human compliance officer always makes final reporting decisions.
  • Business Account Blind Spots: Relaxed commercial limits require ongoing calibration.
